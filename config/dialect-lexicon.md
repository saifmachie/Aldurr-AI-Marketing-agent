# Al Durr Supplies — Dialect Lexicon

Approved Iraqi colloquial terms, cleared for use in an MSA-backbone post.
Grows weekly from `state/amendments.jsonl` dialect-type edits. The copywriter
may use only what is listed here; anything else must be flagged `NEW_TERM`
for review before it enters this file.

Target register: an educated Baghdad professional writing for a B2B
audience — natural Iraqi warmth, not street slang, not literary MSA.

---

## Approved terms

| Term | Reading | Use for | Cleared |
|---|---|---|---|
| ما تنباع | not-sold (colloquial negation) | direct, blunt trust statements | seed — from spec sample post |
| نقطة | "period" / full stop, used as a standalone emphatic | closing a hard claim | seed |
| تسويها | "you do it" (colloquial verb form of تفعلها) | instructing the reader on self-service actions | seed |
| بأي وقت | "at any time" | flexibility / self-service framing | seed |
| مو | colloquial "not" (vs MSA ليس) | short, direct negation in a closing line | seed |
| وين | colloquial "where" | only inside the WhatsApp attribution question, never in ad copy | seed — see spec §1 |
